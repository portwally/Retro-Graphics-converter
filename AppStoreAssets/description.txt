RETRO GRAPHICS CONVERTER

Open, view and convert graphics from the golden age of home computers. Retro Graphics Converter reads pictures from 16 classic platforms - Apple II and IIgs, Commodore 64, Amiga, Atari ST, ZX Spectrum, BBC Micro, MSX and more - straight from their files or from original disk images, and turns them into modern PNG, JPEG, TIFF, GIF or HEIC files.

PICTURES FROM DISK IMAGES
- Open 15 disk image formats: Apple II (2IMG, DSK, DO, PO, HDV, DiskCopy 4.2), Commodore (D64, D71, D81), Amiga (ADF), Atari ST (ST), Atari 8-bit (ATR), BBC Micro (SSD, DSD), ZX Spectrum (TRD), Amstrad CPC, MSX and CoCo (DSK)
- Browse ProDOS folders, DOS 3.3 catalogs and every other file system with real file types
- Pictures on the disk are found automatically
- Search, tick the pictures you want and import them in one go
- Export the raw files from a disk image as well

SUPPORTED PICTURE FORMATS
Apple II / IIgs: HGR, Double Hi-Res, Super Hi-Res (including 3200-color, Apple Preferred Format, 816/Paint, DreamGrafix and PaintWorks)
Commodore 64: Koala Painter, Art Studio, Advanced Art Studio, Hi-Res
Commodore Plus/4 and VIC-20: Hi-Res and Multicolor
Amiga: IFF/ILBM up to 256 colors, Extra-Half-Brite, HAM6 and HAM8
Atari ST: Degas, NEOchrome, Art Director, GFA Artist, Palette Master, Doodle
Atari 8-bit: GR.8 to GR.15, MicroIllustrator
ZX Spectrum: SCR screens
Amstrad CPC: Mode 0 and Mode 1
MSX: Screen 1, 2, 5, 7 and 8
BBC Micro: Modes 0, 1, 2, 4 and 5
TRS-80 and CoCo: block graphics, PMODE, CoCo 3
PC and Mac: PCX, BMP, MacPaint
Modern: PNG, JPEG, GIF, TIFF, HEIC

EDIT THE ORIGINAL PALETTE
- Change any color and see the picture update at once
- Works even on 3200-color Apple IIgs pictures with a palette on every line
- Copy and paste palettes, reset with one click
- Compare the edited picture with the original

CONVERT AND SHARE
- Export to PNG, JPEG, TIFF, GIF or HEIC, or keep the original retro file
- Upscale 2x, 4x or 8x with crisp pixels
- Batch export many pictures at once, without overwriting files
- Rotate, flip, crop, invert and adjust brightness and contrast
- Create a macOS screensaver or a video slideshow from your collection

FAST TO USE
- Drag and drop pictures, folders or disk images
- Thumbnail browser with a filter for each platform
- Zoom, histogram and picture details
- Keyboard shortcuts for import, export, selection, zoom and browsing
