Version 4.5 is a big quality update. Every decoder and disk reader was checked against thousands of real disk images and sample pictures, and many pictures that used to look wrong now open correctly.

NEW
• Keyboard shortcuts: ⌘O Import, ⌘E Export, ⌘A Select All, Delete to remove, ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0 to zoom, arrow keys, Home and End to step through your pictures
• New Atari ST formats: Art Director, GFA Artist, Palette Master and Doodle
• More disk images: Commodore D71 and D81, 40-track D64 and Apple DiskCopy 4.2
• CoCo PMODE 1 pictures and CoCo pictures saved from Disk BASIC
• Browser filters for Atari 8-bit, BBC Micro, MSX, Plus/4, VIC-20 and TRS-80/CoCo
• Palette editing for Apple Preferred Format, the most common Apple IIgs format, and for Amstrad CPC, Atari 8-bit, BBC Micro, Plus/4, VIC-20, CoCo, MSX and more

BETTER DISK SUPPORT
• Apple II disks are no longer mistaken for CoCo disks
• ProDOS folders on .dsk/.do images are now listed, and DOS 3.3 names with spaces are shown in full
• C64 disks show every file (the 8th, 16th, 24th... file was missing) with clean names
• Real CoCo RS-DOS disks, ZX Spectrum TR-DOS and BBC Micro .ssd/.dsd disks are read correctly, including both sides of double-sided disks
• The disk browser shows the right file type for every platform
• Several disk images opened together now open one after another
• Exporting files from a disk keeps folders and never overwrites files

BETTER PICTURES
• Amiga: Extra-Half-Brite colors, masked pictures and short palettes are now correct
• C64 Advanced Art Studio, MSX Screen 7 and 8, MacPaint with MacBinary header, BMP (top-down, 16 and 32-bit) are fixed
• Atari ST high-resolution pictures are shown in black and white, as on the ST
• Fewer false pictures: music and data files on disks are no longer shown as images

EASIER TO USE
• The palette editor follows the line under the mouse, scrolls to it, and resets in one step, even on 3200-color pictures
• The histogram now shows pictures with few colors clearly
• Batch export never overwrites files with the same name
• Rotate, flip and upscale work for every picture
• The info bar shows the real picture size, also after crop or rotate
• Cleaner toolbar labels, a drag-and-drop hint in the empty browser, and updated Help

STABILITY
• Recent Folders open again after restarting the app, and exporting files from disk images and creating screensavers work again
• Whole folders can be chosen in the Import panel
• Results and error messages are now shown in the status bar
• Fixed several crashes and hangs with damaged pictures and disk images, when dropping many files at once, and during movie export
